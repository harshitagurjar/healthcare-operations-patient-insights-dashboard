# 🏥 Healthcare Operations & Patient Insights Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze healthcare operations and patient-related metrics. The dashboard helps monitor patient requests, admissions, refusals, staff performance, patient satisfaction, and bed availability, enabling data-driven decision-making for hospital management.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem
Hospitals generate large volumes of operational data every week. Without an interactive reporting system, it becomes difficult to:
- Monitor patient requests, admissions, and refusals.
- Track patient satisfaction across healthcare services.
- Evaluate staff distribution and morale.
- Monitor hospital bed availability.
- Identify operational bottlenecks and support resource planning.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Build an interactive healthcare operations dashboard.
- Analyze patient requests, admissions, and refusals.
- Compare patient satisfaction across services.
- Evaluate staff distribution by role.
- Monitor staff morale by service.
- Track weekly patient admission and refusal trends.
- Provide interactive filtering using slicers.

---

## 📈 Key Business Insights
- Processed approximately *13K patient requests*.
- Recorded approximately *6K patient admissions*.
- Recorded approximately *8K patient refusals*.
- The high patient refusal count indicates possible capacity or operational constraints that may require further investigation.
- Patient satisfaction varies across healthcare services.
- Staff distribution differs by role, helping identify workforce allocation.
- Weekly trends help identify periods of higher patient demand.
- Bed availability can be monitored to support hospital resource planning.
